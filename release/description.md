**Stream or record World of Warcraft without showing anyone's real name.**

OnAir swaps player names for aliases you choose: in chat, on nameplates, unit and raid frames, tooltips and damage meters, and in the friends, guild, group finder and mail windows. Other addons' text too. End the broadcast and every real name comes back at once, with no reload.

For **Retail** and **WoW Forever**. No libraries.

## Whose names

- **You**: your stage name, on every character you play.
- **Your party and raid**: cast names from a theme, or your own template.
- **Everyone else**: strangers, guildmates, friends, people in lists and chat.
- **Battle.net friends**: real names and BattleTags.
- **Guilds and communities**: yours reads "The Star's Studio", others "Studio 1", "Studio 2"...
- **Pets and minions**: "Owner's Pet", with the owner's alias.
- **NPCs** (optional): a generic label (Enemy, NPC, Boss) or nothing.

The same player keeps the same alias every session. Give your duo partner a fixed one on the Custom page.

## Go live

Click the minimap button or the Deck's live button, use a key binding, or type **/onair live**. Try **Preview** first: everything is masked like live, so you can check your screen. Old chat lines with real names can be wiped when you go live.

## Made for streaming

- **Stream chat**: a clean chat window over yours while live. It shows only what you pick (NPC talk, emotes, loot), with names already swapped.
- **Moments**: a clock starts at 00:00 when you go live. Boss pulls and kills, keys, deaths, levels, achievements and epic loot are marked by themselves, and a key marks your own. Export them as YouTube chapters.
- **Shield**: while live, whispers from strangers wait until you end (with an auto-reply if you like), and their invites, duels and trades are declined. It can hide your zone and minimap too.
- **Camera**: cinematic presets, zoom, field of view, shoulder offset, smooth follow and a slow orbit. Your own camera settings come back after.
- **The Deck**: a small control bar that stays on screen with the UI hidden (Alt+Z).
- **Four looks**: a camera viewfinder, a vision mixer, a film slate or a studio console.

## Names above heads

The game draws names above heads itself, and addons can't change that text. While live, OnAir gives friendly players name-only nameplates, which show their aliases.

For full cover there's **Blank name font** (Hide page): no name above any head, players or NPCs, live or not. The game reads this font only when you log in, so it needs a logout to switch on or off. Friendly NPC nameplates bring NPC names back.

## What the game doesn't allow

- In combat and in dungeons the game hides some names from addons. OnAir can't swap those, so it hides that text instead.
- Blizzard's friendly nameplates inside dungeons and raids can't be changed by any addon.
- The combat log tab isn't changed.

While live, type **/onair check** to look for real names still on screen.

## Commands

- **/onair**: open the console
- **/onair live** and **/onair off**: go live, end the broadcast
- **/onair preview**: preview on or off
- **/onair mark [note]**: mark a moment
- **/onair check**: look for real names still on screen
- **/onair reshuffle**: everyone draws a new alias

Key bindings: Options > Keybindings > OnAir.

## Your settings

OnAir changes a few game settings while live (names above heads, the camera) and puts yours back when you end. What it learns stays in your own saved variables.
